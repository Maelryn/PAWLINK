# Impact and viability

Every number here comes from [`facts.md`](facts.md) (with its source) or is a target we set ourselves, marked as such. No invented statistics.

## The problem is real and measurable

- **Animal bites are a public-health issue.** 121,545 people needed rabies treatment (post-exposure prophylaxis) after a bite in Algeria in 2017; about 80% of these cases came from dog bites ([facts #1](facts.md)). 15 to 20 people still die of rabies every year (2010–2017, [facts #2](facts.md)).
- **Disasters hit animals and the families who live from them.** The August 2022 wildfires cost more than 400 head of livestock and affected the livelihoods of over 6,000 people ([facts #3](facts.md)).
- **Rescue already exists, but it is small and scattered.** One Algerian association reported 73 rescue operations and 32 volunteers for the whole of 2023 ([facts #8](facts.md)). Help depends on who happens to see a Facebook post.

## Who PawLink helps

| Who | What PawLink gives them |
|---|---|
| **People who find a hurt or lost animal** | One place to report it, an immediate "how urgent is it and what do I do now", and a way to follow the help live |
| **Volunteers and rescue associations** | Cases near them, already sorted by urgency, with the right skill asked (transport, foster, food…), and a way to ask for the next step |
| **Vets** | Urgent cases nearby, and a directory where people can find and book them |
| **Farmers in wildfire and flood zones** | An emergency map where everyone sees where animals need help |
| **Moderators and associations** | Fewer fake or duplicate posts: checks on place, photo and reporter, plus confirmation by people nearby |

## First target (our goal, not a measured number)

Start where the team lives and where the 2022 fires hit, then grow city by city:

- **Where:** Algiers and Tizi Ouzou.
- **With whom:** 3 local rescue associations and their volunteers.
- **Goal for the first 3 months:** 500 registered users, and every urgent case seen by at least one helper.
- **What we measure:** the time between a report and the first "I can help", and how many cases end "resolved".

## How it pays for itself

- **Free forever** for people who report and for volunteers.
- **Vets and pet shops pay to be found first:** PawLink Pro, 2,900 DA a month (featured listing, online appointments, replies to reviews, statistics).
- **PawLink never handles money between users** (adoption is free, appointments are paid at the vet).
- **Costs today:** the app runs on free plans (Vercel, Render, Supabase). Moving to paid servers costs a few thousand dinars a month, so **a handful of Pro subscribers covers the servers**; every subscriber after that funds growth.

## What happens after October

1. **Month 1:** launch in Algiers and Tizi Ouzou with the partner associations; collect feedback in the app.
2. **Month 2:** publish the Android app (Flutter, in progress); bring the first vets and pet shops onto PawLink Pro.
3. **Month 3:** improve the AI with real cases reviewed by vets; open the next cities.

We intend to keep PawLink running after the programme.

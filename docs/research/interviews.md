## Summary (Sabrine, once the interviews are in)

The 3 things that came up the most:
1. Difficulty finding adopters: Finding someone willing to adopt an animal in need is often one of the hardest parts of rescue.
2. Access to nearby help: Pet owners and rescuers need easier access to nearby veterinary services and people who can help.
3. Better organization of rescue cases: Having accurate information, including the animal's location and the reporter's contact details, can make rescue efforts easier and more reliable.

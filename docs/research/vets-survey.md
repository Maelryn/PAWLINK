# Vets questionnaire: responses

The questionnaire we sent to vets instead of a spoken interview. One section per vet who answered. First name (or "Dr" + initial) and city only: never a phone number or a full name. Ask permission before quoting anyone.

Answers from pet owners and volunteers go in [`interviews.md`](interviews.md).

## Template (copy it for each answer)

### Dr [initial] · [city] · [type of practice: pets / farm animals / both] · [date]

1. How often are stray, injured or lost animals brought to you?
2. How do you usually hear about an animal that needs help (call, Facebook, someone walks in…)?
3. What makes these cases hard (late arrival, nobody pays, owner unknown…)?
4. Have you seen fake or exaggerated posts about animals?
5. Would you use PawLink to see urgent cases near you? What would stop you?
6. Would you pay a small monthly fee (PawLink Pro, 2,900 DA) to be found first and get online appointments?
